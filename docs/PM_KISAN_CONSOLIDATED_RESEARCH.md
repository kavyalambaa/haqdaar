## 1. Scheme Overview
Scheme: Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi
Type: Central Sector Scheme
Annual benefit: ₹6,000 per eligible family
Payment: Three instalments of ₹2,000

## 3. Major Historical Changes
- Original scheme restricted eligibility to holdings up to 2 hectares.
- Revised 2020 guidelines extended coverage to all eligible landholding farmer families.
- Historical amendments addressed Aadhaar, payment procedures,
  community-held land and land-transfer situations.

## 4. Eligibility Research
- Family definition: husband, wife and minor children.
- Cultivable landholding must be established using applicable land records.
- Scheme exclusions must be checked.
- Land ownership changes require date and mode of acquisition.
- Inheritance must be distinguished from sale, gift and partition.
- State-specific provisions must be considered.

## 5. Exclusions to Verify
- Institutional landholders.
- Constitutional post holders.
- Ministers and specified elected representatives.
- Specified serving and retired government employees.
- Pensioners receiving the specified pension amount.
- Income-tax payers.
- Registered professionals actively practising.

## 6. HAQDAAR Rule Engine Requirements
- Never treat unknown as false.
- Never apply the historical 2-hectare limit.
- Separate speaker land from other family members' land.
- Preserve raw spoken land units.
- Ask follow-up questions when ownership information is unclear.
- Route uncertain cases to manual verification.
- Keep eligibility decisions separate from payment status.
