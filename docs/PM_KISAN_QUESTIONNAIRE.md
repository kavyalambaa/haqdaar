## Section A — Basic information

1. What is your name?
2. How old are you?
3. Which state and district do you live in?
4. What is your occupation?

## Section B — Family information

5. Are you married?
6. Do you have children below 18 years of age?
7. Who are the members of your farmer family as defined under PM-KISAN?

## Section C — Agricultural land

8. Does your family own cultivable agricultural land?
   - Yes
   - No
   - Not sure

9. Whose name is recorded in the land records?
   - My name
   - My spouse's name
   - Joint names of family members
   - Another person's name
   - Not sure

10. How much cultivable land is recorded?
11. Which unit is used locally?
12. Is the land recorded in multiple revenue records or villages?

## Section D — Land ownership history

13. When did the current ownership begin?
14. How was the land acquired?
   - Inheritance after death
   - Purchase
   - Gift
   - Partition
   - Other
   - Not sure

15. If inherited, when did the previous landowner pass away?
16. Has the land record mutation been completed?
17. Did your family transfer any cultivable land after 1 February 2019?
18. Does your family retain any cultivable land after that transfer?

## Section E — Exclusion checks

Ask about the farmer family members covered by the scheme's exclusion rules.

19. Is any relevant family member a former or present constitutional post holder?
20. Is any relevant family member a former or present Minister or specified elected representative?
21. Is any relevant family member a serving or retired government employee in a covered category?
22. Is any relevant family member a retired pensioner receiving a monthly pension of ₹10,000 or more?
23. Did any relevant family member pay income tax in the last assessment year?
24. Is any relevant family member a registered professional who actively practises as a doctor, engineer, lawyer, chartered accountant or architect?

For government employment and pension questions, collect the employee category to check the specified MTS/Class IV/Group D exceptions.

## Section F — Verification

25. Can you provide the applicable land-record details?
26. Do you have the identity and bank details required by the current official process?
27. Is there any information about your land or family that you are unsure about?

## Response handling

Every answer must support these states:
- Yes
- No
- Unknown
- Not applicable

Do not convert unknown into no.
